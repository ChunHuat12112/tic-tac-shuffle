TIC TAC SHUFFLE

VS
Round: the game picks a glowing board. X plays one move, then O replies in the same board. Then the game jumps to another random board. Win a small board to claim that big cell. Two ways to win: SUPER BIG WIN means you claim three big cells in a row, at any time. BIG WIN means every board was decided first and you hold more big cells.
EP: both players gain 1 EP at the end of every round (max 5). You may use one buff per round, before your move.
Convert (2 EP): only while you are behind in big cells. Flip one enemy mark in the glowing board into yours.
Redirect (2 EP): choose which board the next round is played in.
Claim (4 EP): a full board with no winner shows TIE. Claim that big cell for yourself.
