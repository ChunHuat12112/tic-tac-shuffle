Tic Tac Shuffle

X moves, O replies in the same board, then the board shuffles

How to play: The game picks a highlighted small board at random. X plays one move there, then O replies in the same board. That is one round. After each round the game jumps to another undecided board at random, and X starts again. If X's move wins or fills the board, the round ends immediately. Three in a row claims that cell of the big board; a full board with no winner is a draw and is skipped. Claim three big cells in a row to win the game. In vs Computer mode you are X and the computer is O.
